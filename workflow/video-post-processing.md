+ **[SlingSite](https://slingsite.github.io/en/video/)**: Free, unlimited and privacy-respecting video optimizer for the web. No installation needed, the optimization happens in your browser (your files never travel to any server). Upload as many videos as you need. For each one, you will get 3 sizes: mobile, tablet, desktop. For each size, 3 video outputs and 3 cover images: video in webm (VP9 codec), video in mp4 (HEVC codec aka H.265) video in mp4 (AVC codec aka H.264) + poster image in  jpg, poster image in webp and poster image in avif for maximum browser compatibility.
